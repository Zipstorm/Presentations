# Provn.co Analysis

**Date**: 2026-05-20
**By**: Akshay Gupta

Competitive analysis of [Provn.co](https://provn.co) — an AI-powered, skills-based talent platform that replaces resumes with role-specific challenges (the "Talent Draft").

---

## Slide 1: Title

Provn.co Analysis — skills-based hiring through the Talent Draft.

---

## Slide 2: What is Provn.co?

- **Talent Draft** — candidates complete role-specific challenges instead of submitting resumes
- **Candidacy Portfolio** — submissions showcase problem-solving, communication, current skills
- **100% human review** — no algorithmic filtering
- **Job Marketplace** — browse open roles and match to challenges

Tagline: *"Your resume shows where you've been. The Talent Draft shows what you can do."*

---

## Slide 3: By the numbers

- 28,500+ users
- 6,500+ applications submitted
- 100% human-reviewed
- Based in Seattle

---

## Slide 4: Positioning

**Problem they attack**
- Job boards require simultaneous luck (open role, matching title/years, referral)
- Resumes are weak proxies for capability
- Algorithmic screening rejects strong candidates pre-human

**Their wedge**
- Skills-first, demonstrated work over claimed credentials
- No referrals required
- Human-in-the-loop as trust signal

---

## Slide 5: Provn vs. Helix / SeekOut.ai

**Provn**
- Challenge-based application surface
- Marketplace + portfolio for candidates
- Human review as differentiator

**Helix**
- AI-tailored portfolio + resume per role
- Recruiter-side discovery + candidate presentation
- Two-sided: candidates tailor, recruiters source
- AI evaluation augments human review

---

## Slide 6: Threats & opportunities

**Threats**
- Same anti-resume narrative we use
- Skills-demonstration story is candidate-friendly

**Where we win**
- Tailoring scales across every application, not one challenge per role
- Recruiter-side sourcing surface is wider than a marketplace
- AI evaluation scales past what 100% human review can sustain

---

## Slide 7: Open questions

- Monetization model? Employer-paid? Per-hire?
- Conversion rate from challenge → hire?
- Who authors challenges — Provn, employers, or AI?
- Does the human-review claim hold at 10x volume?
- Funding, team, runway?

---

## Slide 8: Takeaways

- Provn validates the skills-over-resume thesis from the candidate side
- Their format is narrow; Helix's tailoring is broader and reusable
- Watch for: pricing reveal, employer logos, scaling proof
- Track in the competitive set alongside other skills-first platforms
